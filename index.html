📖 Present Estates — Quick Reference

Fee Simple Absolute

The largest estate — full ownership with no conditions or limitations. Lasts forever.

Signal words: "To A" or "To A and her heirs"

Fee Simple Determinable

A defeasible fee that ends automatically when a stated event occurs. Grantor holds a possibility of reverter.

Signal words: "so long as," "while," "during," "until"

Fee Simple Subject to Condition Subsequent

A defeasible fee where the grantor may elect to reclaim the estate if a condition is breached. Does not end automatically.

Signal words: "but if … O reserves a right of entry," "provided that … O may reenter"

Fee Simple Subject to Executory Limitation

A defeasible fee that transfers to a third party (not the grantor) upon a stated condition. The third party holds an executory interest.

Signal words: "but if … to B," "so long as … then to B"

Life Estate

An estate measured by the lifetime of the holder (or another person). Followed by a remainder or reversion.

Signal words: "for life," "for the life of"
